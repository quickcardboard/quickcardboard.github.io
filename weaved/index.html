How to play
  1. Drag a letter from your hand into a gap in the board's sequence.
  2. If your placed letter, together with its board neighbors, spells a word, that stretch of tiles clears.
  3. Empty the board and your hand to win.
  4. The ★ wild tile stands in for any letter, but skip it for a perfect solve.
