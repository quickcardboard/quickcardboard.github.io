How to play

  • Find the four hidden four-letter words.
  • Enter words into the grid and submit your guess.
  • Marks a correct letter in the correct position in the row or column.
  • Marks a correct letter in the incorrect position in the row or column.

Navigator and review

  • Go back to view previous submissions.
  • Select multiple tiles and enter notes.
  • Lock tiles to preserve letters.
